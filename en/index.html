PROCESS

How I
work

Every project starts with understanding what’s behind the request. Before building anything, I try to learn about the context, the business, the people who’ll use it, and the specific need to be solved.

  1. 01

    Understand

    I ask questions, review the information available, and try to separate the real need from the first idea of how to implement it.

  2. 02

    Align on scope

    Together, we turn that understanding into priorities, deliverables, dependencies, open decisions, and what’s out of scope.

  3. 03

    Propose

    I present alternatives when it makes sense, explain my decisions, and point out the trade-offs of each option.

  4. 04

    Design and build

    I build the solution in stages, keeping the focus on priorities and reviewing structure, behavior, and details.

  5. 05

    Review and deliver

    We review what was built against the scope, and I leave the documentation and context needed to keep the work going.

“I understand the context, align the scope, propose with sound judgment, and design or build in stages.”
